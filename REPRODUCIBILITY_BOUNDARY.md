# Reproducibility boundary

The archive **preserves the locked documentary classifications** and permits deterministic reproduction of the numerical summaries, robustness diagnostics, interval-valued representation sensitivities, and figures reported from them. It does not claim that live-web documentary classifications are regenerated de novo by code.

Official source titles, issuers, dates, languages, URLs and retrieval/preservation status are recorded in the provenance files. Third-party web documents are not redistributed unless their reuse status permits it. No local snapshot or hash is reported when a legally retained local copy is unavailable.

## Selection-universe boundary
The complete pre-outcome eligible universe beyond the original oldest-four boundary was not preserved before AI-policy outcomes were known. The archive therefore does **not** represent a retrospectively assembled web list as the original pre-outcome universe. Instead, it provides:
- official-source eligibility certificates for all 58 selected institutions;
- jurisdictional official registry/authority sources;
- a retrospective boundary audit for non-traditional and contested HEI types; and
- the preserved pre-outcome selection review where available.

These materials support re-audit of the selected cases and documented boundary decisions, but they do not make oldest-five outcome-independent. Oldest-five is therefore not reported.

## U search boundary
`U` means qualifying public evidence was not located under the documented bilingual protocol by the fixed cutoff. The archive records the actual search surfaces and stopping rule in `data/P2_U_Search_Stopping_Rule.md` and the case-level closure records in `data/P2_Documented_Bilingual_Search_Log_29.csv`. `U` is not interpreted as proof that an internal policy is absent or that an institution is non-compliant.

## Computational boundary
Internet access is not required for numerical reproduction. The reproduction code does not independently re-download live websites, establish implementation quality, infer causal policy translation, estimate sector prevalence, or create documentary states from unstructured web content.
