# Camera-ready repository insertion text

Do **not** insert a public author-identifying repository link into the double-blind manuscript.

After double-blind review has ended and the final GitHub/Zenodo records exist, a camera-ready availability statement can use wording such as:

> The accompanying reproducibility archive preserves the locked documentary classifications and provides the data tables, provenance records, eligibility-audit objects, search-closure records, code, and derived outputs needed to reproduce the reported numerical summaries, sensitivities, and figures. The final archive is available at [GitHub URL] and is permanently archived at [Zenodo DOI].

Replace bracketed placeholders only after the permanent records exist.
