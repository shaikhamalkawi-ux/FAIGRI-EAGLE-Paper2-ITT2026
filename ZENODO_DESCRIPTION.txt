Reproducibility archive for “From National AI Ambition to University Rules: A Bilingual GCC Evidence-Chain Audit.” The archive preserves the locked documentary classifications and reproducibly derives the numerical summaries, robustness diagnostics, interval-valued fuzzy representation sensitivities, and figures reported from them. It also includes official-source eligibility certificates for all 58 selected institutions, jurisdictional registry sources, a retrospective eligibility-boundary audit, the documented U search stopping rule, source provenance, and deterministic reproduction code. The archive does not establish implementation quality, sector prevalence, country ranking, causal policy translation, or absence of non-public institutional rules. The complete pre-outcome eligible universe beyond the original selection boundary was not preserved and is not retrospectively reconstructed as the original universe.
