# Institutional selection protocol

Evidence cutoff: **23 July 2026**.

Eligible units are active, officially recognized/licensed civilian higher-education institutions. Within each jurisdiction, institutions are partitioned into G1 public/general, G2 private/non-government, and G3 public applied/technical/specialized. Within each jurisdiction × stratum cell, order eligible institutions by the current institution's official founding year and select the oldest four. Include the full tie at fourth place. If fewer than four eligible institutions exist in a cell, include all available cases; do not borrow across strata. Direct author-affiliation institutions are excluded before AI-policy outcome searching.

Founding year is a reproducible ordering key only; it is not a proxy for prestige, maturity, quality or representativeness. Sample membership is finalized before outcome coding.

A later consistency review identified a Bahrain G2 implementation error: Kingdom University (2001), already verified as eligible, had been omitted while two 2002 institutions were included. Its 2001 founding year and private-university status are documented in an official BQA institutional review; BQA's higher-education report index, last modified 7 July 2026, lists Kingdom University with a July 2026 compliant institutional review, providing dated pre-cutoff support for eligibility continuity. Reapplying the unchanged rule replaces those two 2002 cases with Kingdom University. This is a rule-consistency correction, not a new selection design.
