# Documented stopping rule for U (unlocated public evidence)

`U` means that a qualifying pre-cutoff public instrument was **not located under the documented bilingual protocol**; it does not mean that no internal policy exists.

For each institutional `U` case, closure required the following steps already represented in the 29-case bilingual search log:

1. Apply the recorded English and Arabic query families to the official institutional domain. Query families include AI policy, artificial intelligence policy, generative AI / GenAI / ChatGPT, and academic-integrity + AI terms, with Arabic equivalents.
2. Check the official policy/governance/regulations library where available.
3. Check official student/faculty handbooks or academic-integrity material.
4. Check official teaching-and-learning / assessment / academic-affairs pages where available.
5. Use regulator/authority pages for eligibility, history, or context; these do not substitute for an institution-level rule.
6. Treat news, training, workshops, research projects and AI-program pages as leads only; they do not satisfy the rule gate by themselves.
7. Close the search only when no qualifying **pre-cutoff** instrument remains after the required official surfaces are checked, and record the closure reason in `P2_Documented_Bilingual_Search_Log_29.csv`.

A later search may recover a source issued before the fixed cutoff, but a post-cutoff instrument is not inserted into the fixed historical comparison.
